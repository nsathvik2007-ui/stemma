Water is constantly recycled between the atmosphere, the land, and the sea through what scientists call the hydrologic cycle. Heat from the sun causes liquid water in oceans and rivers to turn into vapor, which floats upward into the sky. As this vapor cools at higher altitudes, it condenses and forms clouds. Eventually these clouds release their moisture back down as rain, snow, or hail. This water gathers in bodies of water and underground before making its way back to the sea, and the process starts again.
