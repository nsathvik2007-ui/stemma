The French Revolution began in 1789 and fundamentally transformed French society by dismantling the absolute monarchy and the feudal system. Rising discontent among the common people, fueled by widespread food shortages and unfair taxation, eventually led to the storming of the Bastille. Over the following years, the revolutionary government abolished aristocratic privileges and executed King Louis XVI. The revolution also inspired the rise of Napoleon Bonaparte, who would go on to reshape much of Europe through military conquest.
